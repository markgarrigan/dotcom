Story

Luna Coffee has roasted small-batch coffee since 2000, and I've been its web developer since the early 2000s, long before I used GitHub. Today's platform is the fourth version of the site and the third of the admin.

The current version runs the whole online business. Customers shop for coffee, tea, gift boxes, gift cards, drinkware and apparel, then check out for pickup, local delivery (checked by ZIP code) or shipping. Orders are packed, labelled and tracked in the admin, and the local driver delivers them from a phone app. A monthly job writes a Stripe sales report, and product images come from a separate image host.

Over those versions it opened new sales channels for them: online ordering, local delivery, shipping, gift cards and fundraisers.

System, layer by layer

[x] Interface
Express 5 with a small custom file-based router, plus htmx, server-sent events and idiomorph, and Tailwind 4 through Vite.
[x] API
api.lunacafe.com on Express and Sequelize, about 25 models, with a bearer-token client.
[x] Data
MySQL, with Stripe payments and invoices as the source of orders.
[x] Infra
Google Cloud Run, deployed by GitHub Actions on each release. Cloud Storage for images and reports, EasyPost for shipping and Mailgun for email.
[x] Ops
A monthly Stripe sales report that runs as a Cloud Run job, rate limiting, and a customer email at every fulfillment step.

Private tool: Luna delivery app

Luna delivery app

Private · Private tool, shown as illustrations · 2025-08 → now

A phone app for the local driver. It isn't public, so the screens here are drawn from its real templates with invented addresses.

  • ▸Shows every order waiting for local delivery as a numbered stop on a map
  • ▸Pulls those orders straight from Stripe payments and invoices tagged for delivery
  • ▸Drag stops to reorder the route
  • ▸Tap a stop to confirm it's delivered
  • ▸Confirming marks the order delivered in Stripe, with a timestamp

Decisions

  1. 01

    Stripe is the order book

    Orders come straight from Stripe payments and invoices. The app keeps no separate order ledger.

  2. 02

    Pages are just files

    Pages are HTML files with optional data loaders, routed by a small custom handler. Adding a page means adding a file.

  3. 03

    Every fulfillment gets its own path

    Pickup, local delivery (ZIP allow-list, $6 charge), shipping with EasyPost labels across UPS, FedEx and USPS, gift cards, and manual orders. Each has its own panel and its own customer emails.
